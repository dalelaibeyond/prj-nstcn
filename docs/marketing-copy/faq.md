# Questions about products and custom development

Start here when preparing your product or business workflow requirement.

### What does NEXSTACK AI focus on?
Our main product directions are AI companion toys and AI office assistants. Our development work also includes AI Agent workflows.

### Who should contact you?
Business decision-makers can contact us about a product idea, office task or custom AI workflow requirement.

### Can I request custom development?
Yes. Share the product features, software tasks or branding changes you need. The proposed scope and project conditions are discussed before work begins.

### Do you discuss OEM and ODM projects?
Yes. Send us your product and manufacturing requirements. The development scope, production arrangement and commercial conditions need to be confirmed for your project.

### Can I see a demonstration or request a sample?
Contact us about the product you are considering. Available demonstrations, sample options and product details need to be confirmed for that requirement.

### What should I define for a companion toy?
Start with the intended age group, interaction, content and memory requirements. Include the target market and how the product will be evaluated.

### Can an office assistant use company information?
Describe the information and tasks involved. We can discuss the required access, permissions and development approach for your project.

### Can I request a particular deployment arrangement?
Include your hosting and data-handling requirements in the brief. Feasibility, dependencies and costs need to be assessed for the proposed scope.

### What are the minimum order quantity and delivery time?
These need to be confirmed for the product and project scope. Include your expected quantity and target date when you contact us.

### Which certifications apply?
Requirements depend on the product, intended users and destination market. Product-specific documentation and its status need to be verified before a supply commitment.

### How do I start?
Submit the contact form with your company details and requirement. You can also contact us by email or WhatsApp.
