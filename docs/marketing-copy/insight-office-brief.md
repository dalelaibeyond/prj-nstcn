# What to define before developing an AI office assistant

An office assistant project needs a clear description of the work it should support. Start with one task and the people responsible for its output.

## Describe the current task

Write down the steps your team follows today. Identify the input, tools and final output. Explain where people make decisions and what usually causes exceptions.

## Identify the information

List the documents, records or systems relevant to the task. Note who owns the information, who may access it and how it changes. Use a sanitised example for an initial discussion.

## Define the requested support

Describe what you want the assistant to produce or do at each step. Separate a suggestion for a person to review from an action performed in another system. Ask the development team to confirm the available capability.

## Set approval and exception requirements

Decide which outputs need human review. Explain what should happen when information is incomplete, a tool is unavailable or a request falls outside the task. These requirements should be part of the proposed scope.

## Discuss deployment and responsibilities

Share hosting, data-handling and access requirements. Ask who will operate the system, maintain the information and handle changes. Confirm the proposed dependencies and costs for the arrangement.

## Agree the evaluation

Choose representative examples and the expected result for each one. Include exceptions as well as routine tasks. Define the evidence you need to decide whether the proposed assistant meets the requirement.

## Prepare a brief your team can review

Bring the task, information, review points and evaluation examples into one document. A shared brief gives business and technical decision-makers a concrete basis for the development discussion.
