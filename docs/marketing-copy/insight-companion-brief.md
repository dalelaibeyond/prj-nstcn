# How to brief an AI companion toy project

A companion toy brief should explain the intended users and the experience you want to develop. A clear brief also gives the development team a basis for discussing product requirements and evaluation.

## Define the intended users

Write down the age group, intended setting and role of a parent or supervising adult. Include the destination market and any requirements already agreed by your business.

## Describe the interaction

Give a short example of what the user might say or do and the response you expect. Explain which interactions matter most. Ask the development team to confirm which requirements can be supported by the proposed product.

## Specify content boundaries

List the topics and situations the product should handle carefully. Describe your expectations for inappropriate requests, missing information and adult involvement. These expectations belong in the requirements and evaluation plan.

## Discuss memory and information

Explain what the product should remember, who should control that information and how users should be able to change or remove it. Ask for a clear description of the proposed information handling before agreeing the scope.

## Include the physical product

Bring your design, branding, power and connectivity requirements into the brief. If you have a reference product, explain which parts of it matter to your project. Confirm specifications and sample arrangements with the supplier.

## Agree the evaluation

Prepare representative interactions and define what an acceptable result looks like. Decide which product documents and demonstrations you need to review before proceeding.

## Bring the brief to your development discussion

You can begin with a short document covering users, interaction, content, memory and product requirements. Mark unanswered questions so they can be discussed explicitly.
