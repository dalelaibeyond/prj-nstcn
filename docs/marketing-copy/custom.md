# Discuss custom development for your AI project

Tell us what you want to build and who will use it. We can discuss hardware, software and AI development requirements for your proposed project.

## Product and brand requirements

For companion toys or office assistants, share the product concept, intended users and changes you need. Branding, hardware and software requirements are assessed against the project scope.

## Business information and workflows

For an enterprise assistant or workflow, describe the task and the information it needs. Include the tools it should work with and any approvals required.

## OEM and ODM discussions

Contact us about your product specification, branding and manufacturing requirements. The production arrangement, sample plan and commercial conditions need to be agreed for the project.

## How a project discussion starts

1. Share the requirement, target users and intended market.
2. Review the proposed tasks, product features and constraints with our team.
3. Agree the development scope, evaluation criteria and commercial terms before work begins.

## Prepare your brief

Include existing specifications, a target date and any requirements your team has already agreed. For enterprise projects, describe the workflow using a sanitised example.
