# Questions to ask an AI development partner

Before agreeing an AI product or workflow project, ask for a clear account of the proposed work and the evidence behind it. The answers should help your team decide what to evaluate and who will be responsible.

## What has the team actually delivered?

Ask for examples relevant to the work you need. Clarify the team's contribution, the scope delivered and which materials the client has authorised for sharing. A workflow example and a hardware delivery example demonstrate different types of work.

## What is included in the proposed scope?

Request a written description of features, integrations, documents and deliverables. List the assumptions and items that still need assessment. Agree how changes to the brief will be handled.

## How will the work be evaluated?

Discuss demonstrations, sample arrangements and representative tasks. Define the result you expect and the evidence required to review it. For a product, confirm the model and specifications being evaluated.

## Who handles development and production?

Clarify who performs software, hardware and manufacturing work. Ask which activities are completed by the team and which involve other parties. Confirm the responsibilities that apply to your project.

## How will information and access be handled?

For an enterprise project, discuss the information involved, permissions, hosting and operating responsibilities. Ask for the proposed arrangement and the conditions affecting it.

## What documents and commercial conditions apply?

Confirm the product-specific documentation, price basis, delivery arrangements and ongoing support. Record outstanding questions before agreeing a commitment.

## Use the answers to prepare your decision

Bring the evidence, scope, evaluation and responsibilities into a short review document. Your team can then assess the proposed project against its actual requirement.
